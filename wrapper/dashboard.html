Step 3 of 5 — Prepare your job feed

Open hiring.cafe once so AMM can start pulling jobs.
Clicking Launch opens a real Chrome window at hiring.cafe. The site checks that you're not a bot (this can take ~30s). Once you see job listings, close the window and continue. If you already have a hiring.cafe account, sign in — that unlocks the "Save" button in your ranked-jobs list.

This step is required. Without it, your first daily scrape will fail against hiring.cafe's bot check.

What we search for

Every term below is searched on hiring.cafe during each scrape. Mix full titles ("IAM Engineer") and short keywords ("iam") freely.
Tip: the Resume page can suggest terms straight from your CV — and switch between title-style and keyword-style suggestions. Click a term's both / cafe / dice tag to route it to one source.

Blocked companies

Jobs from these companies are dropped before scoring — they never reach your batch. Matches the start of the company name (case-insensitive), so "Google" also blocks "Google LLC".

Job sources optional

Beyond hiring.cafe, pull jobs straight from company career boards — public, official feeds, no login, more reliable. Enter the board slug from a company's careers URL (comma-separated). Leave blank to use hiring.cafe only.
