Step 2 of 5 — Your basics

A few settings so AMM knows what to look for.
You can change any of these later from the dashboard.

Step 3 of 5 — Prepare your job feed

Open hiring.cafe once so AMM can start pulling jobs.
Clicking Launch opens a real Chrome window at hiring.cafe. The site checks that you're not a bot (this can take ~30s). Once you see job listings, close the window and continue. If you already have a hiring.cafe account, sign in — that unlocks the "Save" button in your ranked-jobs list.

This step is required. Without it, your first daily scrape will fail against hiring.cafe's bot check.

Step 4 of 5 — Telegram (optional)

Want batches pushed to your phone too?
AMM's main UI is this dashboard. Telegram is a bonus — it pushes each morning's ranked list to your phone so you can skim over coffee. You can connect it now or from the Telegram card after setup.

Step 5 of 5 — Finish setup

Ready to go.
Clicking Finish saves your settings and schedules the daily scrape. AMM starts running in your system tray; the dashboard will refresh into its normal view. Your first batch is one click away — the Scrape now button on the main dashboard.
